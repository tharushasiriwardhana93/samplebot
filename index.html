About PeraBots

PERABOTS is the premier national robotics competition organized by the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, University of Peradeniya.

Designed for both school and university students, PERABOTS provides a platform to showcase innovation, technical expertise, and problem-solving skills through exciting robotics challenges. As the department's flagship annual event, PERABOTS aims to inspire the next generation of engineers while growing into an internationally recognized robotics competition.

Why PeraBots?

PERABOTS is more than a competition—it's a platform for innovation, collaboration, and engineering excellence. We empower young innovators to transform ideas into reality, strengthen teamwork and problem-solving skills, and develop technologies that create a positive impact on society.

Competition Categories

Choose your track and showcase your innovation

School Category

Step into the world of robotics through an exciting two-phase journey. Participants will first design, program, and test their robots in an online simulation, before bringing their creations to life in the physical competition.

With hands-on workshops throughout the journey, students will build their skills, gain confidence, and get ready to take on the challenge.

phase1(online challenge) – Design and program your robot in Open Roberta Labs and tackle a series of autonomous challenges.

phase2(physical finals) – Build your physical robot, bring it to the arena, and compete in challenging real-world autonomous robotics tasks at the University of Peradeniya

View Rules

University Category

Put your engineering skills to the test through exciting autonomous robotics challenges. Participants will apply their knowledge of robot design, programming, sensing, and navigation while developing valuable technical and teamwork skills.

The competition features two phases, progressing from virtual simulation to real-world implementation.

phase1(online challenge) – Design and program autonomous robots in Webots, completing simulated robotics challenges to qualify for the finals.

phase2(physical finals) – Bring your physical robot, built according to the competition tasks, and compete in real-world challenges at the University of Peradeniya.

Our Objectives

Empowering the next generation of engineers and robotics innovators

Promote STEM

Inspire school and university students to explore Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM).

Discover Talent

Identify promising students and provide opportunities to develop their technical and leadership potential.

Foster Innovation

Encourage robotics enthusiasts to embrace emerging technologies and solve real-world engineering challenges.
